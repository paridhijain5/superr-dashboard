What counts as pressure

Each ball gets a leverage score: how far win probability is expected to swing on that delivery. The top 20% of balls within each innings are high pressure. The realised swing confirms it works:

Average win-probability swing per ball, in percentage points, by pressure tier.

How the rating is built

Every ball is scored against what an average player does in the same situation, then adjusted for the opponent. The rating mixes pressure output (60%), improvement on usual level (15%) and consistency across pressure innings (25%).

In a chase, the median required rate on a high-pressure ball is 9.5, the same as on any other ball. What differs is how balanced the match is: the median gap from 50:50 is 0.17 versus 0.42.
